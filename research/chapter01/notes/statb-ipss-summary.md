## 0. Key structural findings (what is ACTUALLY published online as of 2026-10-22)

1. **Statistical Handbook of Japan 2026** is the newest edition (published September 2026; "The present issue basically contains statistics that became available by April 30, 2026."). Its population figures are therefore as of October 1, 2024 (latest annual population estimates based on the 2020 census).
2. **2025 Population Census (Oct 1, 2025)**: Preliminary Counts released **May 29, 2026** (total 123.05 million); **Basic Complete Tabulation on Population and Households (人口等基本集計) released September 29, 2026** with final (確定値) figures — total 122,972,528. English overview page "POPULATION AND HOUSEHOLDS OF JAPAN 2025" is linked but returns 404 as of access date.
3. **Population Estimates (人口推計)**: monthly estimates are still benchmarked to the 2020 census; the benchmark will switch to the 2025 census from the November 2026 report (per Japanese Population Estimates page).
4. **IPSS Population Projections for Japan**: the **2023 revision (令和5年推計, published April 26, 2023)** remains the newest national population projection round as of 2026-10-22 (confirmed on IPSS English section page, IPSS Japanese main menu, and by the Statistics Bureau's own September 2026 elderly-population release citing 令和5年推計). IPSS household projections have a newer 2024 round (令和6年推計, published April 12, 2024).
